    Got Questions?

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Q.

    How do I book a laundry service?

    You can book directly through our web form on this page, or message us on Facebook Messenger anytime. Fill in your name, contact number, preferred service, and pickup details — we'll confirm your slot within the hour.

    Q.

    Do you offer pickup and delivery?

    Yes! We offer pickup and delivery for residential and corporate clients across Metro Manila. Message us or use the booking form to check if we cover your area.

    Q.

    How long does laundry take?

    Standard service takes 1 to 3 business days. If you need it sooner, our Express Service offers same-day turnaround when items are dropped off or picked up before noon. Rush fees apply.

    Q.

    Do you offer same-day express service?

    Yes. Drop off or schedule your pickup before 11:00 AM and we'll have your laundry ready the same day. An express/rush fee is added on top of the base service price.

    Q.

    What payment methods do you accept?

    We accept GCash, Maya, cash, credit cards, and debit cards (Visa & Mastercard). You can pay on pickup, on delivery, or through our cashless options.

    Q.

    Can you handle delicate and special-care items?

    Absolutely. We offer hand washing for delicate fabrics like lace, wool, and silk, as well as professional dry cleaning for suits, barongs, gowns, blazers, and leather pieces. Just let us know when you book.

    Q.

    What kind of detergent do you use?

    We use premium, eco-friendly, and hypoallergenic detergents. If you have sensitive skin, just let us know and we'll use our special fragrance-free options.

    Q.

    Is my laundry safe with you?

    Yes. All items are inspected and logged upon receipt, and we're fully accountable for every garment we receive. If you have concerns about specific items, just let us know and we'll handle them with extra care.

    Q.

    Is there a minimum order?

    Machine wash has a minimum load of 5 kg (about a small bag). Hand wash minimum is 3 kg. Dry cleaning and specialty items are charged per piece, so there's no minimum for those.
